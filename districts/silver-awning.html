Silver Awning

Ironport’s largest and most densely populated district. Sprawling across the city’s southern reaches. It gets its name from the looming skyland Unus, chained above, casts the southwestern quarter in near-perpetual shadow, earning the district its name. You’ll see shanty buildings stacked on each other, next to an admirable dwelling. This the heart of Ironport’s working class, each pocket teeming with its own customs, dialects, and makeshift shrines.

Notable people

Gaudino ‘Mad Eye’ De Toro

Suave, magnetic, and terrifyingly unpredictable. The rumored crime lord of the infamous Nine Roses. The main crime syndicate in the city. Despised by many leaders but loved by just as many nobles. Little is known of his motives but he almost always gets what he wants.

Adkin Willhelm

Honorable and indomitable. A once hero of Ironport and former veteran of the Firebrands. Famously saved the brother of the King, Prince Bilal’s life. Beloved in the Silver Awning for turning down the king’s many offers of different titles. A humble man from even humbler beginnings.

Captain Aberardus ‘The Turbulent’

Blunt and honorable. This Branded Knight, a part of the Golden Legion division is the Warden of the Ironport Correctional Facility. A veteran of the streets and battlefield alike. Known for his iron will and iron hand literally. He is unyielding in his pursuit of justice.

Hewelin

Soft-spoken but unwavering. The leader of the Lamplighters is respected by locals, tolerated by the Church, and feared by the corrupt bureaucrats. For years now he has headed the orphanage/youth home. Known for their information gathering and lighting of the lamps around the city.
