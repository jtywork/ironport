Rupert Square

Rupert Square is Ironport’s main thoroughfare. It is both a marketplace of trade and a place of deal making. At the city center stands the grand bronze statue of King Rupert Daederic, it gazes towards the harbor proudly. During the day the streets are lined with merchants, guild recruiters, street performers and clergy alike. Night time Rupert Square transforms into a bustling entertainment district. One can find nobles, adventures, and criminals all mingling. A district of duality: trade and temptation, honor and indulgence, spectacle and scandal.

Notable people

Lucien Daniele “The Velvet Lion”

Poised, eloquent, and unflinchingly polite. Lucien runs one of the most prestigious inns in town, The Velvet Chalice. Here the currency is gossip and indulgence.

Persephone Gill

Elegant, articulate and quietly severe. Persephone is the Trade Overseer of The Gilded Exchange. She has a great fire in her and it burns for the GC and wants nothing more than to help The Exchange grow.

Wallace Wormly

Quiet, methodical and effortlessly polite. Wallace runs the Hidden Seamstress, one of the most sought after tailors in town. He wants nothing more than to keep his relationships loyal and his business his own. His eye for detail is unmatched.

Tirian Lilly

Reserved, deeply perceptive, and enjoys the company of a nice hammer and anvil over a person. The proprietor of The Singing Anvil. Rumors are his blades sing to the wielders. Preserving his integrity and craft are his motivators.

Kymos Nahl

Flamboyant, witty, and magnetic. Kymos is the owner of The Opaline Aetherium. A boutique filled with magical items, clothes, tools, and more. Once a former Nefnore lecturer he wants to keep magic accessible to all.
