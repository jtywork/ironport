Ivory Pavilion

Separated from the grime of the lower districts, this district separates itself with high walls, and marble streets. Instead of chaotic roads, the district is dissected by a network of pristine canals. Here lies the main operations for the 3 main influences in Ironport. The Basilica of the Eternal Flame, Nefnore Star-Spire, and Consortium Headquarters. Here aristocrats and nobles bump shoulders and share secrets.

Places of note

Basilica of the Flame

TempleMap no. 2

Seat of the Church of the Eternal Flame. Its platinum domes are wreathed in fire that never dies.

Crimson Barracks

BarracksMap no. 3

Quarters of the Order of the Purified Flame, the Basilica's armored defenders.

Nefnore Star-Spire Campus

AcademyMap no. 4

Nefnore Academy's campus around the Star-Spire, whose crown glows at night with tuned aether.

Gilded Consortium Headquarters

HallMap no. 5

The Gilded Consortium's marble-and-gold seat of power. The President's Terrace overlooks the whole city.

The Ivory Walk

GardenMap no. 6

A canal-side promenade of marble patios and fountains, lined with campus-sponsored shops.

Notable people

Provost Thalia Griffiths

Measures, meticulous and principled. Thalia’s goals are simple. Keep admissions merit-forward. She receives constant pressure from nobles and aristocrats to allow more influence from outside the Academy.

Cardinal Edmund Corvell

Polished, precise, and utterly self-assured. He wants the CoEF to be the ever-present fixture in Ironport, more than it already is. With desires of huge reform for the city.

President Keres Grimm

Patient, and unrelentingly analytical. Grimm’s desires are very straightforward but meeting these goals aren’t. Wanting total mercantile control of Ironport and eventually all of Andala. He wants his will to endure.
