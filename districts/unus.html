Unus, the Chained Skyland

Of the three skylands closest to the city, only Unus is anchored to the mainland by enormous arcane chains. This tethered skyland forms the heart of the Sky-Forge District's mining operations, and its shadow falls across the southwestern quarter of the Silver Awning. Skyferries from the Iron Rise carry workers and cargo up to its mines and camps.

Places of note

The Iron Rise, upper dock lift station, no. 49 on the map
Where skyferries from the Iron Rise unload on Unus.
Hollow Crown Mine mine, no. 50 on the map
Ironport's old iron mine, still stretching deep into Unus.
Veinreach Quarry zephyrite workings, no. 51 on the map
The crown jewel of Zephyrite extraction, heavily guarded by Ironport Industries.
Cliffbreaker's Respite tavern, no. 52 on the map
A lawless camp of scavengers, prospectors and rogue miners.
The Horizon-Eye Reliquary observatory, no. 53 on the map
An ancient structure on Unus's jagged edge, now an observatory for Nefnore scholars and skypriests.
