Castle Grounds

A top the gradual ascent from the sea, the Main Keep of Ironport Castle stands a jagged silhouette against the coastal sky. Made from meteoric stone and raw materials harvested from Skylands and Unus. The walls are made of weather-darkened rock from the early days of Skyland mining alongside sections that look startlingly new, where fresh renovations have integrated raw Zephyrite directly into the masonry. Deep, opalescent veins of the crystal run through the ramparts.

Places of note

Ironport Castle (Main Keep) castle keep, no. 1 on the map
Seat of King Al'Thor Dederic. A keep of meteoric stone veined with Zephyrite, crowning the city's highest rise.

Notable people

King Al’Thor Dederic
Wise, weary, and cautious this king of 87 wants to secure a peaceful succession for his family and the realm.
Prince Rand Dederic
Charismatic, ascetic and intensely zealous. Wants to help Ironport come under church rule.
Princess Raenessa Leclair
Charming, cold, has a knack for finding the truth. Wants to establish the throne as an Oligarchy with the backing of the Gilded Consortium.
Prince Bilal Dederic
The King’s brother, an intellectual who is aloof, but calculating. Wants to help the crown come under more influence with the Grand Magi from Nefnore.
