Sky-Forge District

Situated on one of the higher ground levels within Ironport’s gradual city ascent, the Sky-Forge District lies just below the looming chained skyland Unus. This district is a plethora of foundries, processing towers and manufacturing alike rooted in stone and steel. Smoke-belching chimneys and rune-lit refineries line the skyline of this district where the skyland looms over all. Here is the Iron Rise or the magical lifts that hauls cargo connecting the city to the mining outposts of Unus.

Places of note

Ironport Industries Headquarters hall or headquarters, no. 16 on the map
The nerve center of Ironport Industries, humming with power drawn from processed Zephyrite.
The Iron Rise lift station, no. 17 on the map
Twin lift-towers where skyferries carry workers and cargo up to Unus.
The Chained Bastion fortress, no. 18 on the map
A fortress built into the Great Chain. Its crews keep the chain's arcane stabilizers intact.
Skybreaker Depot refinery, no. 19 on the map
Arcane furnaces that refine ore and Zephyrite. Flares of wild magic are not uncommon.

Notable people

Tharok Vellgrave
Calm, persuasive and calculating. Tharok wields his words as weapons as the Executive Orthane of Ironport Industries. The main processing plant for Zephyrite in Ironport puts this business as the only true rival to The Gilded Consortium.
Yedra Clastworn
Gruff, stern and pragmatic. Yedra is the Chief Foreman of the Chained Bastion. The massive anchor that keeps Ironport bound to the skyland above. She is blunt with her words but deeply loyal to her crew.
Corwin Fen
Excitable, awkward and naive. He is an Zephyrite Crystallurgist (Arcane Engineer) one of the youngest and brightest minds in Zephyrite refinement. He wants to achieve a breakthrough that cements his name in history.
Selvara Kaelith
Stern and uncompromising, Selvara is the High Warden of the Skybreaker Depot, the main Zephyrite ore processor. Her rare Ifrit bloodline has tied her life to fire and steel. From the neighboring mountain home of the dwarves Hammerhearth. She wants to protect her workers from both corporate interference and magical catastrophe.
