Iron Gate

The Iron Gate stands sentinel on Ironport’s eastern wall, where the cobbled streets of Rupert Square narrow into disciplined order. The district smells of oil, steel, and parchment. Rows of barracks and drill yards line the main avenue, where recruits of the Golden legion march in perfect formation. This same district houses the King’s Row where scribes attend to citizenship records, trade licenses, and custom ledgers. It also houses the City Watch division of the Golden Legion. The citizens that live in this district more often than not work in this district as well.

Notable people

Censorate Brünn Aevers

Measured, steady, and unflinchingly fair. The Chief Censorate of The King’s Row. He prioritizes order not for its own sake but because he’s seen how quickly a city can unravel without it. Wants to maintain the integrity of Ironport.

Commander Yulgrim Tuvash

Stoic, disciplined and deeply principled. His reputation precedes him as a stern but uncruel man. He wants to preserve the Branded Knights and what it stands for, or at least what he was told it stood for.

Gate Overseer Jeric Mourn

Brittle, stern, and prone to outbursts of anger. As the Overseer of the Iron Gatehouse, Jeric commands his gate with intimidation. He sees all traffic in and out of that gate, and he knows that.
