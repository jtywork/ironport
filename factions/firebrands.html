The Firebrands

“Freedom, fire, and fame.”

They are a chaotic-neutral confederation of performers, revolutionaries, and heroes-for-hire. They have no central base or hierarchy, and members operate independently or in temporary crews. Though once famous for their daring rescues and anti-slavery missions, they are now equally known as entertainers and storytellers.

Symbol: A flaming handprint or flame-emblazoned mask.
