Artisans' Concord

“Earned, not given.”

A guild of all the artisan crafts except for the shipwrights. Tired of being squeezed by the Gilded Consortium for control of the markets and Ironport Industries control on raw materials. The Concord helps regulate pricing floors and material-sourcing agreements for the artisans of Ironport.

Symbol: A hammer and quill crossed over an open ledger.

Where to find them

  • Hammer & Ledger Hall
  • The Singing Anvil
