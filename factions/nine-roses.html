The Nine Roses

“To Bloom is to Bleed.”

The Nine Roses are Ironport’s largest and most organized criminal syndicate. They operate across all districts but maintain headquarters in the Silver Awning. The syndicate deals in smuggling, racketeering, monster trading, and underground bloodsport arenas.

Symbol: Nine thorned roses entwined around a spiked chain.

Where to find them

The Lost Oasis
