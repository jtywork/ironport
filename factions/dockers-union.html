Dockers' Union

A collective representing dock workers, skyport crews and laborers across Burnished Harbor, led by Captain Ehtel Hanley. The Union's rank and file increasingly idolize Captain Elias “Twice-Lucky” Vane, who became the face of the recent dock strikes.
