The Shackled Tide

“Bound by coin, sealed in silence.”

Underground slaver network hidden beneath the bustling commerce of Burnished Harbor. The Shackled Tide operates as Ironport’s most secretive and vile slaving operation. Though outlawed by the city law, their trade flourishes through silence, fear, and powerful allies.

Symbol: A simplified anchor where the flukes are shaped like open manacles.
