The Branded Knights

“Marked by Duty, Bound by Steel.”

Ironport’s standing military and peacekeeping force. They are divided into four sub-orders, they operate across land, sea, and sky, upholding the stability of the city-state. Each knight must bear the mark of their sub-order’s insignia at all times.

Orders

Golden Legion

The elite urban army and city guard.

Silver Corsairs

Naval enforcers and pirate hunters.

Bronze Falcons

Expeditionary forces abroad.

Onyx Talons

The network of spies and inquisitors.

Symbol: An anvil viewed from the side; its material changes by order.
