The Red Hogs

Brash, brutal and blatantly hired muscle: the Gilded Consortium's private mercenaries in the Silver Awning. Members wear armor adorned with hog parts (tusks, hides, even skeletal snouts). They patrol Consortium warehouses, and locals fear them.
