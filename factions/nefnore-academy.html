Nefnore Academy of the Arcane

“In the weave, the eye opens.”

The premier institution for magical study, located in the Ivory Pavillion. Its scholars map the Leylines and study the properties of Zephyrite. While respected, they are viewed with suspicion by the CoEF. Students come from all walks of life via tuition or magical sponsorship.

Symbol: A radiant eight-pointed star encircled by floating script: an open eye over a shimmering void at its center.
