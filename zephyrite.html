Zephyrite

Zephyrite is a rare and opalescent crystal found only on skylands throughout Andala, with rich deposits located near Ironport. Of the three skylands closest to the city, only Unus is anchored to the mainland by enormous arcane chains. This tethered skyland forms the heart of the Sky-Forge District’s mining operations. The other two skylands float 5–15 miles westward over the Bronze Expanse, and efforts to mine them are currently underway.

Zephyrite is a cornerstone of Andalan magical infrastructure. When refined, it channels levitation, propulsion, and arcane conductivity. Making it the primary fuel for skyships, magical constructs, and arcane industry.

Who holds a stake

Ironport Industries

Ironport Industries oversees most Zephyrite mining and processing on Unus.

Freelancers

Freelancers and sanctioned expeditions now push into the other two skylands.

Gilded Consortium

Gilded Consortium pressures for more industrial-scale refinement and streamlined use.

Hullwrights' Assembly

Hullwrights’ Assembly champions craftsmanship and adaptive use over mass production.

Nefnore Academy

Nefnore Academy studies Zephyrite’s arcane harmonics and celestial properties. Some skyborn cultures treat Zephyrite as a gift of the heavens, linked to prophecy and starlight.
