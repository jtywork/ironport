Ironport Symbol Catalog

This catalog covers every symbol used across the Ironport project: map symbols, landmark glyphs, faction and guild sigils, noble house seals, and holy symbols. Each entry has a stable ID so samples can be requested and revised by number (for example, "generate M-01 through M-08").

Canon descriptions from the source documents are reproduced as written. Where the documents leave a symbol undefined, the entry is marked Undefined and needs a decision before generation.

Shared style rules

These rules apply to every symbol so the set reads as one family.

  • Map symbols and landmark glyphs: Drawn in the manner of a 17th-century French engraved city plan: sepia ink linework, no gradients, hatching for shade. Each is legible at 24 px on a map and holds up at 128 px in a legend. They are drawn monochrome first. Color, where used, is a flat wash matching the quarter colors on the city plan.
  • Sigils and holy symbols: Heraldic and emblematic, and each must work as a single-color stamp (a wax seal, a banner, a tabard). A full-color version is secondary. Named materials (platinum, gold, silver, onyx) set the tincture of the full-color version.
  • Frame: Map symbols have no frame. Sigils sit on a shield, roundel, or free field only where the canon description implies one.

Suggested generation order

  1. Style anchors: M-02, M-07, M-23, S-06, and H-05. This is one of each kind, used to lock line weight and rendering before generating the rest.
  2. The rest of the map symbols (M): These are needed first for the city plan.
  3. Sigils (S) and house seals (H): These are most visible to players.
  4. Holy symbols (G) and landmark glyphs (L).
  5. Undefined entries, once they have descriptions.
