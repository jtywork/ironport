The Pantheon

The Church of the Eternal Flame is the predominant faith of Ironport, but gods of many natures are worshipped in the city, though some are frowned upon or misunderstood.

Mount Celestia

Mount Celestia is not merely a plane but a physical hierarchy of five distinct peaks, arranged in a descending spiral from right to left, representing the balance of the cosmos.

Center Peak: Licitum (The Summit of Law)

Bahamut (Lawful Good) – God of Justice, Nobility & Law.

Symbol: A Platinum Dragon’s head facing left, wrapped in a crest-shaped flame.

The highest peak, crowned in eternal, golden daylight. Here lies the Platinum Citadel, a fortress of law where Bahamut presides. The slopes are orderly, with marble paths and perfectly manicured gardens.

Upper Right Peak: Magna (The Peak of Life)

Dura (Neutral Good) – Goddess of Sun, Nature & Agriculture.

Symbol: A circle with six outwardly radiating points, with a hammer in front of it.

A lush, verdant peak that teems with untamed but benevolent life. It is said that the sun never sets on Magna, and the harvest is eternal.

Upper Left Peak: Kalos (The Peak of Passion)

Eilistraee (Chaotic Good) – Goddess of Beauty, Freedom, Dance, & Moonlight.

Symbol: A full moon behind the silhouette of an unclad Drow woman with flowing hair.

This peak is perpetually bathed in silver moonlight and starlight. It is a place of wild cliffs, hidden glades, and music that echoes on the wind.

Lower Right Peak: Neutrum (The Fulcrum)

Asgorath (Neutral) – God of Knowledge, Creation & the Arcane.

Symbol: An 8-pointed star with an eye in the middle; the iris contains a void like the night sky.

Kelemvor (Lawful Neutral) – God of the Dead.

Symbol: A skeletal arm holding up scales in balance.

A peak of gray stone and crystalline spires. It is here that souls are weighed, and the raw fabric of magic is woven.

The Foothills: Bas (The Shifting Mist)

Leira (Chaotic Neutral) – Goddess of Deception, Illusions & Lies.

Symbol: A downward-pointing triangle containing swirling mist.

The lowest point of the range, shrouded in a perpetual, thick fog. The terrain here constantly shifts; paths that lead up the mountain one day may lead off a cliff the next.

The Leylines

The world of Andala is wrapped in invisible, torrential rivers of arcane energy known as Leylines, flowing beneath the earth, through the oceans and across the skies. Nefnore's scholars map them. Common folklore holds that skylands like Unus float because they are fragments of Mount Celestia, and that sailing high enough will lead to the gods.
