House Moss

The Alchemists

House Moss controls the vast hydroponic gardens on the Pavilion roofs and produces much of Ironport's medicine. It has declared for none of the three heirs.

Head of house
Lady Ianthe Moss
Interests
Medicine, Alchemy, Botany, Public Health
Device
A green ivy vine encircling a mortar and pestle
In the succession
Undeclared
