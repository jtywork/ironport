Calendar and Holidays

The year runs through four seasons of three months each. The present year is 1487.

Months

Spring (The Season of Awakening)

Aquis (The Month of Waters/Melt)
The thaw of the skychains and the spring rains.
Ventis (The Month of Winds)
The gale season; skyships move fastest now.
Floris (The Month of Blooms)
The planting season; gardens in the Pavilion bloom.

Summer (The Season of Ardour)

Solas (The Month of Sun)
The longest days; associated with Dura.
Ignis (The Month of Fire)
Peak heat; the Sky-Forge is at its most intense.
Serenus (The Month of Stillness)
The "Becalmed" month; humidity is high, winds die down.

Autumn (The Season of Decay)

Aureus (The Month of Gold)
The harvest and the turning of the leaves.
Umbris (The Month of Shadows)
The skies turn grey; the days shorten significantly.
Nebulas (The Month of Mists)
Heavy fog rolls in; the Veil between worlds is thinnest (Leira's time).

Winter (The Season of Silence)

Glacias (The Month of Ice)
The canals freeze; the first snows fall.
Noctis (The Month of Night)
The sun barely rises; the city relies on lamplight.
Astris (The Month of Stars)
The skies clear of clouds, revealing the cosmos (Asgorath's time).

Days of the week

  • Duras (Sunday)
  • Moonas (Monday)
  • Wardas (Tuesday)
  • Dracas (Wednesday)
  • Ferras (Thursday)
  • Leiras (Friday)
  • Vors (Saturday)

Holidays

Spring (The Season of Awakening)

The Unbinding (New Year)

Date: 1st of Aquis

Deity: Secular / Elemental

Marks the first day of the official thaw. In Ironport, this is celebrated by the "Unchaining Ceremony," where the massive heating coils on the Skyland Chains are powered down for the season.

Citizens wear a simple cord or ribbon around their wrist for the week leading up to the holiday. On the morning of The Breaking, they cut the cord and burn it.

The Silver Dance

Date: 15th of Ventis

Deity: Eilistraee (Goddess of Freedom & Moonlight)

A night celebrating freedom and wind. Since Ventis is the windy season, the music carries furthest on this night. In Rupert Square, the Gilded Stage hosts open-air performances, and the Firebrands often use this night to mock authority figures through song.

Wearing silver masks and dancing until dawn; a noble might dance with a docker without scandal on this night.

The Green Gift

Date: 1st of Floris

Deity: Dura (Goddess of Nature)

The planting festival. In the Silver Awning, long tables are set up in the streets with free food provided by House Moss and the Church. Farmers from the Iron Greens bring the first seedlings to be blessed.

Hanging flower wreaths on doorposts to invite prosperity.

Summer (The Season of Ardour)

The Shield Raising

Date: 1st of Solas

Deity: Bahamut (God of Justice)

A celebration of Law, Order, and the Monarchy. The Golden Legion parades through the Iron Gate, and the Basilica of the Flame lights all its braziers at once. It is a day where debts are settled and pardons are sometimes granted by the King.

Citizens wear platinum or silver ribbons pinned to their chests.

Forge Night

Date: 15th of Ignis

Deity: Secular / Industry

The "City’s Birthday." It celebrates the industrial might of Ironport. The Sky-Forge does not sleep; instead, they add chemicals to the smokestacks to turn the smog harmless and colorful (purples, greens, and golds) for one night.

Ironport Industries gives workers a bonus (or an extra ration of grog), and fireworks are launched from the Hullwrights' docks and around the city.

Autumn (The Season of Decay)

The Gilded Gala

Date: 1st of Aureus

Deity: Secular / Wealth

The start of the "Social Season" for the Ivory Pavilion. The Gilded Consortium hosts a massive masquerade ball that determines the social hierarchy for the coming year.

The "Coin-Toss." Wealthy merchants travel to the lower districts and toss handfuls of minted copper coins to the crowds—a display of charity that the Firebrands despise.

The Masque of Mirrors

Date: 13th of Nebulas

Deity: Leira (Goddess of Deception)

A chaotic festival of pranks, illusions, and deception during the foggiest month. Nothing is as it seems. It is a favorite of The Copper Crows, as petty theft is considered "bad luck to punish" on this day.

Telling elaborate lies that must be "believed" until midnight. Bakeries sell "Roulette Pastries" (some sweet, some spicy, some filled with colored dye).

Winter (The Season of Silence)

The Silent Passing

Date: 1st of Noctis

Deity: Kelemvor (God of the Dead)

A day to honor the dead. The city goes quiet. No forges run in the Sky-Forge; no ships leave Burnished Harbor.

Fasting until sundown, then floating small paper lanterns down the Pavilion canals (or into the harbor) for those lost.

The Eye-Opening

Date: 30th of Astris

Deity: Asgorath (God of Arcane/Knowledge)

The final day of the year. Nefnore Academy opens its gates to the public for displays of illusion and wonder. As the clock strikes midnight, the Star-Spire pulses with light to ring in the new year.

Lighting a single blue candle in the window to "guide wisdom home" for the coming year.
